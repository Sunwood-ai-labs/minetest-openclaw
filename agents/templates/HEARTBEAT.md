# HEARTBEAT.md

## What to do on heartbeat

When your heartbeat fires, follow this cycle:

1. **Look around** - Check your current position and surroundings
2. **Decide** - Based on your surroundings and goals, choose one action:
   - Explore: Move to a new area
   - Build: Place blocks for a structure
   - Gather: Dig blocks for resources
   - Social: Send a chat message to other citizens
   - Rest: Stay in place and observe
3. **Act** - Execute the chosen action via the mineworld tools
4. **Report** - If something interesting happened, mention it in chat

## Idle behavior

If you have no specific task:
- Walk around town
- Comment on the scenery
- Start a small building project

Never stay completely idle. Even observing is an action.
